# Idle/Incremental Game Market: Trends, Gaps and Opportunities for a New Indie Title (as of Oct 2026)

> **Method note for the report writer:** Every direct page fetch in this session (howtomarketagame.com, newsletter.gamediscover.co, appmagic.rocks, kotaku.com, wnhub.io) was refused by the session's network proxy. All findings below therefore come from search-engine result summaries of those pages, not from reading them in full. Figures are attributed to the URL the search tool tied them to, but they could not be checked against the full text. Treat exact numbers as "reported by / attributed to" rather than verified. Third-party sales tools (Gamalytic, raijin.gg, AppMagic) are **estimates** and are marked as such. Research date: 2026-10-16.

## 1. Why are incrementals "in vogue" in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
The current wave is driven by a run of cheap ($5–$10), short, highly polished Steam incrementals that YouTubers love (Gnorp Apologue in Dec 2023, Rusty's Retirement in Apr 2024, Nodebuster in 2024), plus free "click for Steam items" phenomena (BANANA, Bongo Cat) that showed huge numbers of Steam users idling. Chris Zukowski (How To Market A Game) has argued that hits followed BANANA's virality, that these games can be built in months, and that supply still lags demand. Hard genre-level counts (tag growth, releases per year) were not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Zukowski / HTMAG (Nov 4, 2025):** incremental games "have been around for years," but BANANA's viral success "seems to have introduced many players to the genre," and "dozens of hit idle games have followed." He adds that such games "can typically be developed in a few months." (Search summary of the post; the full text could not be opened.) — [How To Market A Game, "The optimistic view that indie games are in a golden age right now"](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/11/04/the-optimistic-case-that-indie-games-are-in-a-golden-age-right-now/)
- **Zukowski via a secondary source:** "idle/incremental games convert well, because Steam's audience is hungry for them." This is a second-hand restatement of his survey work. — [Steam Page Analyzer, "How to Get Steam Wishlists"](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-to-get-steam-wishlists)
- **Developer view on supply versus demand:** in an 80.lv piece, incremental developers said people are looking for incremental games but "there's not that much supply yet." They also describe a community where developers openly share wishlist counts and marketing tactics. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/how-to-get-attention-for-your-indie-game-in-a-crowded-market)
- **BANANA (2024):** a free-to-play Steam clicker released in April 2024 that drops tradable Steam Marketplace items. It peaked at **252,171 concurrent players**, and the developer acknowledged a bot problem. Most items sell for a few cents; the record sale was **US$1,378.58**. — [GamingOnLinux (Jun 2024)](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2024/06/hundreds-of-thousands-of-people-are-now-clicking-a-banana-on-steam/); [Wikipedia: Banana (2024 video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana_(2024_video_game)); [MakeUseOf on item-farming games](https://www.makeuseof.com/steam-item-farming-games/)
- **Bongo Cat (2025):** a free desktop companion in which a cat taps along as the user types or clicks. SteamPulse lists about **158.6K live players, a peak of about 193.9K, and 96% positive reviews**. A Vietnamese outlet citing SteamDB reported a peak above **101K concurrent players on Apr 8, 2025**. Stmstat shows about **164K average players in July 2026**. Sensor Tower's VGI ranks it #1 among most-played free games. — [SteamPulse](https://www.steampulse.org/game/3419430); [Phong Vũ](https://phongvu.vn/cong-nghe/game-vo-danh-mien-phi-gay-sot-tren-steam/); [stmstat](https://stmstat.com/jp/app/3419430); [Sensor Tower VGI top charts](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/top-charts)
- **Desktop-strip trend:** coverage of Rusty's Retirement said it "seemingly created a new trend of games that run at the bottom of players' screens." — [TechRaptor](https://Techraptor.net/gaming/news/rustys-retirement-sales); [Wikipedia: Rusty's Retirement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rusty%27s_Retirement)
- **The "short incremental" wave:** reviewers frame Nodebuster as a "short and sweet" modern incremental in a grindy market. Laura Michet puts it at about **3 hours**. A ResetEra thread says Digseum "seems to have emerged following the success of Nodebuster" with a very similar UI and feel, and groups it with Magic Archery and Gnorp Apologue. Players on Steam forums ask for idlers that "don't draw out the experience into hundreds of hours." — [The Mancunion (May 2025)](https://mancunion.com/2025/05/08/nodebuster-review-a-short-and-sweet-incremental-game/); [Laura Michet](https://blog.lauramichet.com/played-nodebuster/); [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/bite-sized-incremental-games.1087230/); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3107330/discussions/0/4695658043402096617)
- **Streamer/YouTube effect:** GameDiscoverCo (Jan 2024) wrote that Gnorp Apologue's "unexpected upgrades and swift 'scaling' endear the game to YouTubers." — [GameDiscoverCo, "How this solo-dev 'incremental' game hit 100k+ sales!"](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-this-solo-dev-incremental-game)
- **AppMagic (2026 report on Steam idle games):** recent Idle releases typically show "strong D1 numbers, usually in the 40% to 70% range, followed by a predictable drop." Its revenue leaders include Spirit City: Lofi Sessions (>$5.3M), Cast n Chill (about $6.8M), The Farmer Was Replaced (>$4.5M) and Scritchy Scratchy (>$3M). All figures are estimates. — [AppMagic, "Idle Games in 2026"](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **2026 titles are showing up on Steam review rankings:** Steam250's incremental chart is topped by Bills Must Be Paid (2026) and includes Berry Bury Berry and Scritchy Scratchy (2026), alongside Bongo Cat, Shelldiver (2025) and Nodebuster (2024). — [Steam250 incremental tag](https://steam250.com/tag/incremental)
- **Editorial buzz (opinion, not data):** a June 2026 listicle calls Dwarf Eats Mountain "the 2026 breakout" mining incremental and describes Gamblers Table as the most-wishlisted incremental of its launch window. — [Top Incremental Games, June 2026](https://www.topincrementalgames.com/guides/top-10-incremental-idle-games-june-2026)
- **No genre-level standout at Next Fest:** GameDiscoverCo's Oct 2025 and Feb 2026 Next Fest reviews said co-op games dominated the top 10, with no mention of incrementals among the leaders. One developer blog says the idle game Desktop Defender was one of the most-played demos of Oct 2025 Next Fest. — [GameDiscoverCo Oct 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-october-2025s-steam-next); [GameDiscoverCo Feb 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)

### Inferences
- The wave has two separate engines:
  - **(a) Premium short incrementals** ($5–$10, 3–15 hours) spread by YouTube "number-go-up" videos.
  - **(b) Free "background" or desktop idlers** (BANANA, Bongo Cat) that win huge concurrent player counts but monetize poorly or controversially.
  - A small premium title should study (a) and borrow the "runs while you work" framing from (b).
- Copycats appear quickly: Digseum followed Nodebuster within months. A popular sub-format such as the "node/skill-tree clearing arena" fills up fast, so a newcomer needs a distinct hook rather than a re-skin.
- The high early engagement AppMagic reports (D1 40–70%) suggests idlers are good at holding players at first. Whether that turns into sales depends on the Steam page and on video creators picking the game up.

### Gaps
- No SteamDB or official figures were found for year-by-year growth in releases or followers on the "Incremental", "Idler" or "Clicker" tags. This is the biggest missing piece of quantitative evidence.
- GameDiscoverCo's genre-level view of incrementals beyond single-game case studies could not be retrieved because the newsletter is paywalled and fetching was blocked.
- The full Zukowski argument, including any data on idle conversion rates, could not be read.

## 2. Revenue and sales evidence for small-team incrementals

### Takeaway
Breakout solo or tiny-team incrementals at $5–$7 have sold roughly 100K–550K copies (estimated gross of about $0.9M–$3M+). The biggest 2025–26 "idle-adjacent" hits (cozy fishing, lofi focus, programming automation, scratch-offs) reach an estimated $3M–$7M. Free desktop idlers can attract enormous player counts while earning very little.

### Cited Findings
- **(the) Gnorp Apologue (Myco, solo, Norway; Dec 2023, $7):**
  - The developer confirmed **100K copies on Jan 6, 2024**. GameDiscoverCo reported **>120K in about a month** and ranked it the **#4 new Steam game of Dec 2023 by copies**.
  - Buyers were **51% US, 7% UK, 6% Germany and 6% Canada**, and the game was English-only at the time.
  - The developer intended "a niche game, with weird gameplay, for a small audience."
  - — [GameDiscoverCo (Jan 2024)](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-this-solo-dev-incremental-game); [Simon Carless on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/simoncarless_how-did-the-gnorp-apologue-sell-120k-copies-activity-7155318508615024641-XHcR); [gnorp.dev, "100k Players"](https://gnorp.dev/news/11-100k-Players/)
  - Gamalytic **estimate**: about **543.7K copies (range 353K–734K)** and gross of about **$3.1M ($2M–$4.1M)**. — [Gamalytic](https://gamalytic.com/game/1473350)
- **Rusty's Retirement (Jordan Morris / Mister Morris Games, solo; Apr 26, 2024, about $7):**
  - Sales: **100K in 5 days** and **200K by May 14, 2024**. GameDiscoverCo reported **almost 330K Steam copies by late summer 2024**.
  - GameDiscoverCo estimated net revenue at about **$5 per unit** against the ~$7 list price, after refunds, launch and seasonal discounts, and the regional pricing mix.
  - It went viral on social media for its concept of farming in the bottom half of the desktop.
  - — [GameDiscoverCo, "How Rusty's Retirement idle-farmed"](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rustys-retirement-idle-farmed); [GamenGuide](https://www.gamenguide.com/articles/105189/20240502/rustys-retirement-idle-farming-simulator-sells-100k-copies-in-5-days.htm); [Automaton (JP), 200K](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20240514-293317/); [Automaton (JP), 300K+](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/rustys-retirement-20240828-308175/)
- **Nodebuster (2024):** raijin.gg **estimate** as of Aug 27, 2026: about **338.5K copies** and about **$860.3K gross** (medium confidence), with **97% positive across 14,031 reviews** and about 43.5K wishlists. The implied average price of roughly $2.50 per copy points to a very low price point or heavy discounting. — [raijin.gg](https://raijin.gg/app/3107330/Nodebuster)
- **Bongo Cat (Irox Games / Marcel Zurawka; free, cosmetic Steam items):**
  - A report citing Eurogamer says the developer still considers it **unprofitable**, despite huge player counts.
  - Reported monthly net revenue: **$2,000 (Mar), $4,050 (Apr), $3,800 (May), $2,800 (Jun)**. The developer said it was mainly a test of the Steam item marketplace.
  - **Conflict:** a SteamDataAI weekly pulse (Feb 2026) lists **$271K** from purchases. The scope and date differ, so the two figures do not reconcile.
  - — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-48117); [SteamDataAI weekly pulse, Feb 15–21, 2026](https://steamdata.ai/blog/steam-weekly-pulse-feb-15-21)
- **Melvor Idle (Games by Malcs):**
  - In Oct 2021, the game had **>600K downloads across Steam, App Store and Google Play**, when Jagex Partners signed a **publishing partnership**. This was **not an acquisition**: the developer kept ownership and creative control, and Jagex handled marketing, localisation, tech support and QA.
  - Melvor Idle 2 was announced in 2025 as **self-published**. A Malcs Steam statement says talks with Jagex were put on hold after Jagex itself changed owners in Feb 2024.
  - — [Jagex news](https://www.jagex.com/news/jagex-announces-partnership-to-publish-melvor-idle); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/jagex-to-publish-runescape-inspired-idle-game-melvor-idle/); [GamingOnLinux, Melvor Idle 2 (Apr 2025)](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/04/popular-idle-game-melvor-idle-is-getting-a-sequel-with-melvor-idle-2-bringing-some-huge-new-features/); [Steam discussion, Melvor Idle 2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218350/discussions/0/598525226050946280/)
  - A third-party page claims a Melvor Idle 2 demo on Oct 19 and Early Access in 2026. This is unverified. — [tideward.app](https://tideward.app/melvor-idle-2/)
- **Cast n Chill (Wombat Brawler; Jun 2025):** AppMagic **estimate** of about **$6.8M** and **>550K copies**. It was the #2 most-wishlisted game on Steam's Idle tag. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **Spirit City: Lofi Sessions (Mooncube; Apr 2024):** AppMagic **estimate** of more than $5.3M revenue and more than 450K copies. The search summary called these figures inconsistent. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **The Farmer Was Replaced** (programming and automation idler): AppMagic **estimate** of more than $4.5M. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **Scritchy Scratchy (Funday Games; Mar 2026):** AppMagic **estimate** of more than $3M and more than 650K copies. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **Unnamed Space Idle:** free to play. Gamalytic lists it, but its numbers (possibly about 745K "copies" and about $2.5M) came from a garbled table and are **unreliable**. — [Gamalytic search result](https://gamalytic.com/game/1473350)
- **Gamblers Table (greenpixels):** the developer reports wishlists going from **0 to about 10K in three weeks**. About **75% of Steam page views came from itch.io** after the web build reached #8 on itch's popular tag, with Reddit adding more. The developer said the link between itch views and wishlists "was not coherent." — [itch.io devlog](https://greenpixels.itch.io/gamblers-table/devlog/945763/wishlists-0-to-10000-in-just-three-weeks-)

### Inferences
- The realistic outcome range for a well-executed, short, premium solo incremental is wide. A notable hit is about 100K–350K units at about $5 net each, or roughly $0.5M–$1.7M. A breakout is 500K or more units.
- The cozy or "productivity" idle segment (Spirit City, Cast n Chill, Rusty's) earns more per hit than "pure numbers" incrementals. That suggests a bigger audience for "idle as ambience" than for "idle as optimisation puzzle."
- The Farmer Was Replaced reaching about $4.5M shows an audience for **programming and automation idle hybrids**, which overlaps with the Zachtronics and Factorio audience.
- These are survivorship examples. No median outcome for incrementals was found, and most releases sell far less.

### Gaps
- No verified sales data was found for Increlution, Progress Knight (or Progress Knight Quest), Ballionaire or Unnamed Space Idle.
- No first-party developer postmortem with full revenue and wishlist figures was found for Nodebuster.
- GameDiscoverCo's breakdown of how Rusty's and Gnorp were marketed (timelines, wishlists at launch) could not be read.

## 3. Platform strategy (web-first, Steam, mobile, Next Fest)

### Takeaway
The proven path is to prototype on the web (itch.io or Galaxy.click) to test the core loop and build early community, then ship a premium Steam version with a demo. Mobile is a secondary port (Melvor) rather than the launch platform for premium indies. Kongregate no longer works as a launchpad.

### Cited Findings
- **Kongregate** closed to new game submissions in **2020**. Existing games remain playable. — [Wikipedia: Kongregate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongregate)
- **Galaxy.click** describes itself as an open-source site for finding incremental games and socialising. It is now a hub for web incrementals, including Progress Knight and its derivatives such as Progress Isekai. — [galaxy.click](https://galaxy.click/); [Progress Knight comments](https://galaxy.click/comments/519?sort=top); [Progress Isekai](https://galaxy.click/play/507)
- **itch.io as a wishlist funnel:** the Gamblers Table case above (about 10K wishlists in 3 weeks, roughly 75% of page views from itch). — [itch.io devlog](https://greenpixels.itch.io/gamblers-table/devlog/945763/wishlists-0-to-10000-in-just-three-weeks-)
- **Melvor Idle** launched across Steam, iOS and Android, with more than 600K downloads by 2021. — [Jagex](https://www.jagex.com/news/jagex-announces-partnership-to-publish-melvor-idle); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1518963622)
- **Increlution** ships on Steam with a free demo and is in **Early Access** with 11 chapters and about **220 hours of unpaused content**. It is a roguelite-inspired, time-management incremental. — [Steam: Increlution](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1593350/Increlution/)
- **Next Fest baselines (GameDiscoverCo):**
  - **Oct 2025:** the top 5% of demos gained 390 followers, the top 10% gained 173, and the median gained 18.
  - **Feb 2026:** GameDiscoverCo puts the true median at nearer 200 wishlists across more than 3,500 demos, while Zukowski's self-reported survey median was 806. The gap reflects survey self-selection.
  - — [GameDiscoverCo Oct 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-october-2025s-steam-next); [GameDiscoverCo Feb 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)
- **Steam volume:** Kotaku (2026) reports that **21,388 titles** had already been added to Steam in 2026, more than in all of 2025, with about 2.5 months of the year left. It links the surge to AI-generated and "vibe-coded" releases. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steam-valve-slop-ai-genai-vibe-coding-pc-game-releases-2000746749)
- **Practical lesson from a Godot itch-to-Steam port:** start the Steam page earlier for more wishlist time, and test the exported build on a clean machine before launch. — [GamineAI postmortem](https://gamineai.com/blog/i-shipped-a-godot-game-to-steam-full-postmortem)

### Inferences
- **Suggested sequence for a solo developer:**
  1. Release a 20–40 minute free web prototype on itch and Galaxy.click, then post it on r/incremental_games.
  2. If engagement is good, open the Steam page right away and link it from the web build.
  3. Release a Steam demo and enter Next Fest.
  4. Launch at $5–$8.
  5. Optionally port to mobile later, as Melvor did.
- Incrementals suit both web prototyping and Steam demos because the core loop shows up within minutes, and because demos fit Steam's interactive "Playing" signals. The Desktop Defender anecdote suggests idle demos can rank well on "most played" even when they do not top wishlist tables.
- Rising Steam release counts make external traffic (itch, Reddit, YouTube) more important, not less.

### Gaps
- No data was found on how far "Incremental"/"Idler" Next Fest demos beat or trail the median.
- No figures were found on mobile revenue for premium indie incrementals, or on how many Steam incremental hits ported successfully to mobile.

## 4. Price points and business models (premium, DLC, F2P)

### Takeaway
The breakout premium incrementals cluster at **about $5–$8** (Gnorp $7, Rusty's about $7, Nodebuster low single digits). Melvor shows the one-time-purchase plus paid-expansion model working over years. F2P on Steam succeeds at reach (BANANA, Bongo Cat, Unnamed Space Idle) but either earns little or draws "scam"/gambling criticism.

### Cited Findings
- Gnorp Apologue sold at **$7**. — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-this-solo-dev-incremental-game)
- Rusty's Retirement sold at about **$7 list and about $5 net per unit** after refunds, discounts and the regional mix. — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rustys-retirement-idle-farmed)
- Nodebuster's estimated gross per copy is about $2.54, implying a very low price and/or heavy discounting. This is an estimate. — [raijin.gg](https://raijin.gg/app/3107330/Nodebuster)
- Ballionaire, a pachinko roguelike with incremental money scaling published by Raw Fury, lists at **about $12.34**. It holds 85% positive across about 1.86K reviews, with some criticism of limited content and unclear explanations. — [Steam: Ballionaire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2667120/Ballionaire/); [Wikipedia: Ballionaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballionaire)
- Melvor Idle and Melvor Idle 2 use **one base purchase, no microtransactions, and paid expansions** that raise the level cap. — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/04/popular-idle-game-melvor-idle-is-getting-a-sequel-with-melvor-idle-2-bringing-some-huge-new-features/)
- Wikipedia notes that microtransactions and ads are common in the genre's mobile and F2P versions. — [Wikipedia: Incremental game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incremental_game)
- BANANA and other item-drop games were criticised as **scams or NFT-like**. The developer denied any scam, Ponzi or crypto link. — [MakeUseOf](https://www.makeuseof.com/steam-item-farming-games/); [Wikipedia: Banana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana_(2024_video_game))
- Bongo Cat's cosmetic-item model reportedly made only $2K–$4K a month net in early 2025, and the developer called it unprofitable. A later source conflicts with this. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-48117)

### Inferences
- For a short (3–10 hour) premium incremental, about $4.99–$7.99 looks like the sweet spot. Content-heavy hybrids such as Ballionaire can go to about $10–$13 but face "not enough content" criticism.
- A Melvor-style paid expansion fits long-form RPG idlers. Short incrementals would do better with a sequel or a cheap content DLC than with live ops.
- Steam players appear to accept free games with optional cosmetics (Bongo Cat's reviews are 96% positive) but not pay-to-progress. This is an inference; no specific review-bombing case over idle monetization was found in this session.

### Gaps
- No documented case was found of an idle game being review-bombed on Steam over IAP or monetization.
- No reporting was found on Valve enforcement against item-farming games after mid-2024.
- No data was found on refund rates for short incrementals.

## 5. Saturated themes versus underserved niches

### Takeaway
Community and market evidence is thin and mostly anecdotal. Visible clusters (likely crowded) are:
- the Nodebuster-style "clear the board / skill tree" short incremental
- mining/digging incrementals
- RuneScape-like fantasy skilling RPG idlers
- cozy desktop-strip companions (pets, farms, fishing)

Commercial hits in 2025–26 increasingly come from **hybrids with a clear non-idle hook**: programming (The Farmer Was Replaced), pachinko roguelike (Ballionaire), scratch-cards and gambling (Scritchy Scratchy, Gamblers Table), fishing ambience (Cast n Chill), focus/productivity (Spirit City).

### Cited Findings
- **Clone clusters:** Digseum emerged after Nodebuster with a "very similar UI and game feel." Short incrementals are often "little more than one big tree and a reason to fill it" (Nodebuster, Snakecremental). — [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/bite-sized-incremental-games.1087230/); [Top Incremental Games, skill trees list](https://www.topincrementalgames.com/lists/incremental-games-with-skill-trees)
- **Mining incrementals** are already a visible subgenre: Dwarf Eats Mountain (2026), To The Core and Digseum. — [Top Incremental Games, June 2026](https://www.topincrementalgames.com/guides/top-10-incremental-idle-games-june-2026); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3107330/discussions/0/4695658043402096617)
- **Desktop companions** now form a distinct category with many entries, such as NoSlack Pets: Lo-Fi Paws and Desktop Fishing, and directory sites list "best desktop companion games." — [wasdland (Oct 2026)](https://www.wasdland.com/games/desktop-companion/); [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/best-desktop-companion-steam-games)
- **Hybrid hits** (all estimates): The Farmer Was Replaced (programming) >$4.5M; Cast n Chill (fishing) about $6.8M; Spirit City (focus tool) >$5.3M; Scritchy Scratchy (scratch-offs) >$3M. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **Roguelite and time-management meta-progression:** Increlution has a long Early Access run with 220 hours of content. — [Steam: Increlution](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1593350/Increlution/)
- **Community complaints (individual itch.io commenters, not consensus):**
  - prestige resets that "feel like a downgrade"
  - themes "not really incorporated into the mechanics"
  - loops that are "too linear/whack-a-mole"
  - — [itch.io comment](https://itch.io/post/7179955); [itch.io comment](https://itch.io/post/5230152)

### Inferences
- **Probably crowded:** reskinned short skill-tree arena games in the Nodebuster mould, mining/digging, generic fantasy skilling idlers in the Melvor mould, and pet or farm desktop strips.
- **Apparently underserved:** for each idea below, no hit was found in this session that owns it.
  - **Idle plus narrative mystery or horror**, where the theme drives mechanics: upgrades reveal the story and the prestige reset is part of the plot. This answers the "theme not integrated" complaint.
  - **Idle plus deckbuilder or roguelite runs** with short sessions. Ballionaire shows demand for "build engine, watch it go," but it was criticised for thin content.
  - **Idle plus programming or logistics puzzles** beyond The Farmer Was Replaced.
  - **Idle plus management sim with a desktop-strip presentation in a non-farm theme**, such as a shop, aquarium, observatory or lighthouse.
  - **Prestige designs that feel like gains rather than losses.**
- Treat these as hypotheses to test with a web prototype rather than proven gaps.

### Gaps
- No r/incremental_games "what do you want to see" or "overdone themes" thread could be retrieved, because Reddit did not surface in search and fetching was blocked.
- No tag-level saturation data (releases per theme) was found.

## 6. Tooling and tech commonly used

### Takeaway
Web incrementals typically use JavaScript/TypeScript with **break_infinity.js** (numbers up to 1e9e15) or **break_eternity.js** (tetration scale). Unity developers have a C# port (BreakInfinity.cs), and Godot 4 has a GDExtension port (break-nihility). No adoption statistics were found.

### Cited Findings
- break_infinity.js is a speed-first decimal library for numbers beyond 1e308, up to about 1e9e15, and has a C# port for Unity (Razenpok/BreakInfinity.cs). — [npm: break_infinity.js](https://npmjs.com/package/break_infinity.js)
- break_eternity.js is its successor and handles numbers up to 10^^1e308. — [jsDelivr: break_eternity.js](https://www.jsdelivr.com/package/npm/break_eternity.js)

### Inferences
- For a web-first plan, either JS/TS (wrapped for Steam with Electron or Tauri) or Godot 4 (which exports to both HTML5 and desktop) supports the path in section 3. Godot's single codebase plus big-number plugin is an attractive fit for a solo developer.
- Desktop-strip or overlay games need transparent, always-on-top windows, which narrows engine choice. That is not verified here.

## 7. Risks

### Takeaway
The main risks are:
- discoverability in a Steam flooded by record release volume, including AI-generated "slop"
- fast cloning of any successful format
- refunds on short games, given Steam's 2-hour refund threshold
- weak monetization of free or item-based models, which also carry a "scam" stigma
- player fatigue with shallow prestige loops

### Cited Findings
- More games were added to Steam in 2026 than in all of 2025 (**21,388**, with about 2.5 months remaining), which Kotaku ties to generative-AI and "vibe-coded" releases. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steam-valve-slop-ai-genai-vibe-coding-pc-game-releases-2000746749)
- The median Next Fest demo gains very few followers or wishlists: 18 followers in Oct 2025, and about 200 wishlists per GameDiscoverCo's estimate for Feb 2026. — [GameDiscoverCo Oct 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-october-2025s-steam-next); [GameDiscoverCo Feb 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)
- **Fast cloning:** Digseum followed Nodebuster. — [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/bite-sized-incremental-games.1087230/)
- **Refunds:** a community comment claims Steam refunds automatically under 2 hours of play within 2 weeks. Valve's actual policy is that refunds are *requested* within 14 days and under 2 hours of play; that correction comes from background knowledge and was not checked this session. GameDiscoverCo counted refunds among the factors cutting Rusty's per-unit revenue from about $7 to about $5. — [GamineAI / community discussion](https://gamineai.com/blog/i-shipped-a-godot-game-to-steam-full-postmortem); [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rustys-retirement-idle-farmed)
- **F2P item-model stigma and bots:** BANANA's bot problem and the "scam"/NFT accusations. — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2024/06/hundreds-of-thousands-of-people-are-now-clicking-a-banana-on-steam/); [MakeUseOf](https://www.makeuseof.com/steam-item-farming-games/)
- **Huge reach does not mean revenue:** Bongo Cat had more than 100K concurrent players yet was reportedly unprofitable. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-48117)
- **Quick drop-off after launch** is typical for idle releases. — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026)
- **Thin-content criticism** hit even a published hybrid (Ballionaire). — [Wikipedia: Ballionaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballionaire)

### Inferences
- **Short games and refunds:** keep the game short (3–8 hours) but put the "hook moment" and visible scaling inside the first 30–60 minutes, so players are invested before the 2-hour refund window closes.
- **Pricing:** a low price ($5–$7) both lowers refund motivation and matches the price-anchored hits.
- **Defending against clones:** speed (a short dev cycle), a strong art and theme identity, and early community ownership via the web build and Discord matter more than mechanics, which are easy to copy.
- **Asset-flip idlers:** the AI-driven flood makes a recognisable visual identity and a clear GIF-able hook (like Rusty's desktop strip) more valuable.
